# Add-on Pack: `domain/consulting` (Stage 3+)

> **Purpose:** Enables a factory to act as an operational consultant and auditor to member factories across a multi-agent fleet.

---

## 1. Capabilities & Added Roles
- **Surveys Lane (`surveys`):** Executes daily multi-factory audits and computes two-tier quality metrics.
- **Delegate Lane (`delegate`):** Conducts cross-factory communications and onboarding gap-closing interviews.
- **League Tables:** Evaluates first-pass yield, lead times, and queue dwell ratios across surveyed factories.

---

## 2. Injected Artifacts & Procedures
- `docs/measurement-procedure.md`: Standardized two-tier ISO 9001 survey procedure.
- `evidence/scores/`: Historical score archives and diff tracking.
