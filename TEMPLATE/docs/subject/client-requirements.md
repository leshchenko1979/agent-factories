# Subject Documentation: Client Requirements & Value Register

> **Client Principle:** Every process runs for a client. Every product must deliver explicit, verifiable value.

---

## 1. Canonical Products & Target Clients

| Product | Target Client | Client Problem Solved | Success Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Factory Template (`TEMPLATE/`)** | New Factory Operators | High friction, ad-hoc architectures, lost state | Bootstraps production-ready factory in $<5$ minutes with passing audit gates. |
| **Consulting Advisories (`evidence/scores/`)** | Member Factory HQs | Hidden bottlenecks, high dwell time, low first-pass yield | Detects queue dwell time $>50\%$; recommends actionable process fixes. |
| **Growth Stages Map (`docs/growth-stages.md`)** | Technical Leadership | Unpredictable scaling roadblocks | Classifies factory maturity accurately across Stages 0–4. |
| **Insights Case Studies (`docs/stories/`)** | Engineering Community | AI hype vs reality; lack of empirical agent architecture literature | Publishes grounded, receipt-backed operational case studies. |
