|---|---|
| Launched | `2026-11-15T06:03Z` |
| Payload | `/tmp/audit-2026-11-15.json` |

The audit ran against a box under sustained I/O contention (load 10–16 against four cores), so
per-gate durations in this round are a **lower bound on a contended box**, not an exclusive-box
measurement. No gate budget was re-derived on that basis — see the §5.2 note in §Gate Status.

Every figure comes from the surface that owns it: systemd for units, the filesystem for sizes,
`tools/ledger.py` for ledger state, `tools/subject_anchor.py` for the documentation gate,
`tools/insights.py` for the insights ledger, each factory's own `evidence/ledger.jsonl` for
per-day close counts, and the daemon's own `cron_jobs` / `cron_job_runs` tables for cadence.

### The 7-day window, stated exactly

The window predicate is `now − 7d ≤ ts ≤ now`, and the **baseline instant had to be recovered
rather than assumed**. The prior round's artifact stamps its ledger-size read at
`2026-11-14T06:02:58Z`, but no 7-day window ending at that instant reproduces the five published
figures. Scanning the day in 30-minute steps, the instant that reproduces **all five** is
`11-14 00:00–01:00Z`. This round therefore uses `11-14T00:30Z` as its baseline.

That is the same class of defect the prior round recorded as its error 3: a total carried forward
without its window. Naming the predicate and the instant is what makes the checksum possible.
