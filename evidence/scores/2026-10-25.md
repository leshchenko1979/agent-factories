## Method

**Scores are carried, Tier-2 is measured.** The 19-cell scorecard for each factory is carried from
prior run evidence, re-added in this round's own arithmetic in both directions, and labelled as
carried. What is re-derived at this round's instant: each member's ledger monotonicity and row
count, the age of its newest close, the share of close rows carrying the canonical `rework=`
trailer, and each member's tree state — all through `tools/audit.py`'s own `parse_ledger()` and its
own trailer predicate, never a second implementation.

**Why Tier-2 does not move a cell.** The trailer share and the close-age are *evidence about*
`S3` and `A2`, not the criteria themselves. Converting them into movement would make the scorecard
a function of whichever ledger happened to be read that day, and the flat series exists precisely
because the cells are stable while the evidence beneath them moves. The figures are reported so a
reader can see the evidence drift without the score moving — which is what a flat series with
moving evidence means.

**Patrol populations come from the patrol.** 23 legs, 0 NOT-RUN, **26 problems**: stall-census 5,
dispatch-delivery 9, undispatched-ruling 6, criterion-paths 3, kit-drift 2, driverless-lane 1. Board
populations were read through the search API's `total_count`, never a `--limit` list.

**The audit runs in its own cgroup.** `systemd-run --user --unit audit-1025`, `RuntimeMaxSec=7200`,
because the previous round's audit was killed by a daemon restart that took its whole cgroup with
it. Payload `/tmp/audit_1025.json`, rc `/tmp/audit_1025.rc`.
