## Implied Template Law & Cross-Factory Learnings

1. **Self-improvement conversion into tracked issues (P27):**
   *Pattern:* InferHub Watch demonstrates that findings from recurring cycle scans only translate to permanent score increases when immediately converted into numbered issues and dispatched to persistent worker lanes in the same turn.
   *Template Impact:* Codified in `TEMPLATE/roles/triage.md` and `docs/best-practices.md` §P27.

2. **Ontology & Single-Writer Bootstrapping Velocity:**
   *Pattern:* Miidas gained +3 points within 24h by addressing Day-1 Mandatory criteria (`ONTOLOGY.md` + ADR 0016 for `CLIENT_KIND` / slot single-writer).
   *Template Impact:* Confirms that Day-1 Mandatory criteria (L1, L2, L3, M1, M4) provide the highest leverage during the Operational phase before Volume-Earned metrics can be measured.
