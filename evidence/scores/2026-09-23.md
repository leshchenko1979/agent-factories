### 🔴 1. Meta-factory — the gate-budget drift GREW while its repair item was open (#141, this lane's)

**Observed.** `gate_budget.budget_staleness()` over `registry/gates.json` at HEAD
`d62a81b99e7e2c7b6f7803919666a84d2061ee28` returns **9 of 47 declared entries STALE:bytes**.

joined the set: `tests/test_close_verify_count_declared.py` and
`tests/test_rework_relative_revision.py`. The other seven are those HQ named.

**Mechanism.** A declared basis names the blob it was measured at; the blob moves and the basis
silently stops describing the test that runs. `tools/gate_budget.py` never re-derives and never
refuses, by design — the values are the process owner's (n=574 PART 5).

**Fix.** Re-derive all nine at HEAD in ONE batch, `budget_sec = margin_x × measured_sec` with
`measured_sec` measured fresh under the current runner form, and rewrite the manifest in the same
commit. Already accepted by this lane as **board #141**; it is the next work item, not a new one.

**Why it is red rather than yellow.** The set is **growing faster than it is being repaired**, and
one member of it (`tests/test_audit_rates.py`) already exhausted its cap once and returned
UNKNOWN — a basis that no longer describes its test is a DATA fact, and whether the cap still
contains the gate is a TIMING fact the audit reports separately.

permits a sampled read at that ratio. Every criterion score is a live read taken this run or a

artifact without saying so.

come from **its own Tier-1 artifact of 2026-09-23** where one exists (all four do), and from a live

**What this run did NOT do.** No member repo was edited, no member artifact was written, no member
