### 🔴 1. Three Member Factories — Re-Sync `tools/hygiene.py` From the Template (stale vendored copy)
- **Observed Symptom:** `tools/hygiene.py --audit` fails in **Infra Factory** and **AI AntiSpam**, falsely reporting `Status: DEGRADED (FAIL)` during their own self-audits.
- **Underlying Mechanism:** The scratch-scope fix already exists in the template — commit `4dafb9d` (2026-09-18 01:54Z, in HEAD) makes the gate glob only `/tmp/<repo-name>-*`, the namespace the factory owns. The member copies predate that commit and glob `/tmp/oc-*`, `/tmp/test-*`, `/tmp/vds-*`, which match **3,240 items** today (3,158 of them `oc-snap-*` directories from OpenCrabs dev tooling). A factory is being failed for litter it did not create.
- **Evidence this run:** `vds-servers`, `ai-antispam`, `miidas` → `tools/hygiene.py` contains no `NAMESPACE` symbol (stale); meta-factory `tools/hygiene.py` **and** `TEMPLATE/tools/hygiene.py` → `NAMESPACE`-scoped (fixed). The meta-factory's own gate matches **0** items in `/tmp/agent-factories-*`, proving the fixed scope is clean.
- **Recommended Fix:** Re-vendor `tools/hygiene.py` from `TEMPLATE/tools/hygiene.py` (or cherry-pick `4dafb9d`) in the three member repos. Any genuinely-owned extra namespace is declared with `--scratch-glob`, never by widening the list to a prefix somebody else uses.
- **Expected Impact:** Restores gate integrity in three factories; a self-audit stops going RED for another lane's litter.

### 🔴 2. Reaper Coverage Gap — `/tmp/oc-snap-*` Is the One Pattern the Weekly Cleanup Does Not Reach
- **Observed Symptom:** `/tmp` holds **13 GB**, of which **3,158 `oc-snap-*` directories** and a **1.14 GB `oc-db-ro.db`** come from OpenCrabs dev tooling. Every gate that globs `/tmp/oc-*` fails fleet-wide because of them.
- **Underlying Mechanism:** A weekly reaper **does exist** — `/usr/local/bin/vds-cleanup.sh` (root cron, Sundays 03:00) — but its `/tmp` coverage cannot remove this litter. `find /tmp -type f -atime +7 -delete` matches **files only**, and the snapshots are directories. Its explicit pattern list covers `/tmp/oc-art*`, `/tmp/oc-artifact*`, `/tmp/oc-verify*`, `/tmp/oc-rel-*`, `/tmp/oc-deploy` — but **not `/tmp/oc-snap*`**, and not `/tmp/oc-db-ro.db`. The snapshot dirs are named for the tools that make them (`oc-snap-oc-deploy-*`, `oc-snap-oc-smoke-*`).
- **Recommended Fix:** Add `/tmp/oc-snap*` and `/tmp/oc-db-ro.db` to that pattern list in `vds-cleanup.sh`, and make the tooling remove its own scratch on exit.
- **Expected Impact:** Reclaims ~13 GB on the shared host and removes the litter that trips every `/tmp/oc-*`-globbing gate fleet-wide.
