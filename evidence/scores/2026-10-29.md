## Method

**What was measured, and what was carried.** The scorecard is carried from the 2026-10-28 round,
cell for cell, and re-verified by re-adding both directions rather than by re-testing: per-family
71 + 38 + 79 + 64 + 57 + 41 = **350**, per-factory 67 + 61 + 58 + 58 + 55 + 51 = **350**,
`350 / 456 = 76.7544 %`. Both sums reproduce in `python3`; neither was performed in prose. Carrying
is the method's design, not a shortcut, and its cost is advisory 10: nineteen identical rounds is a
measurement that has stopped discriminating.

**What was re-derived fresh.** Tier-2 ledgers (five of six — see the absence below), the subject
anchor, the cadence leg, the daemon's cgroup state, brain-file metrics, compaction rate, harness
binding, member worktree cleanliness, and the run's own gate audit. Each carries the instant it was
read beside it, because a single read is a sample and never a state — the daemon's memory figure in
advisory 1 moved from 72 % to 99 % across three rounds and is reported as a range for exactly that
reason.

**Cost is printed, never scored** (owner order 2026-10-03). Figures come from the ledger's `run`
rows through `tools/audit.py::parse_ledger`, never from the internal `usage_ledger`, which measures
