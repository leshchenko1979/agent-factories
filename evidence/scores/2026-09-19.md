### 10.1 The freshness predicate is defective — one cron table, mixed timezones

§1's verdicts compared **wall-clock "today" in UTC** across jobs standing at **different
clock positions**. The box keeps mixed timezones in a single `cron_jobs` table, so the *same*
`cron_expr` means a different slot per row (live read, `mode=ro` URI):

| Job | `cron_expr` | `timezone` | Effective slot (UTC) | `last_run_at` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `inferhub-self-audit-daily` | `0 9 * * *` | Europe/Moscow | 06:00Z | 2026-09-19T06:01:33Z |
| `infra-surveys-daily` | `0 9 * * *` | Europe/Moscow | 06:00Z | 2026-09-19T06:01:35Z |
| `miidas-hq-daily-trigger` | `0 9 * * *` | **UTC** | **09:00Z** | 2026-09-18T09:15:01Z |

At this run's measurement instant — **2026-09-19T06:20:59Z**, commit `7e5506a` — the two MSK
jobs had already fired and Miidas's UTC job had not: its 09-19 slot was **2 h 39 m 01 s in
the future** (`next_run_at` 2026-09-19T09:00:00+00:00, `enabled=1`). **A slot that has not yet
arrived cannot have been missed.**

The second half of the defect: the **artifact-date proxy measures report generation, not
process execution.** Miidas's 09-18 run *did* execute — its ledger row 23
(2026-09-18T09:27:22Z, `run`, `internal-self-audit`, `outcome=accepted gate=all-pass`). Its
newest artifact is 09-17 because its declared writer runs `tools/audit.py --stamp`, which
appends a ledger row and **writes no file** (`tools/audit.py:451`–`473`); `--report` is what
writes `evidence/scores/<date>-self-audit.md` (`:444`–`448`). Its own SKILL.md:295–296 briefs
the pacemaker with `--stamp` and only `--stamp`. A declared writer whose invocation cannot
produce the declared artifact is a **template-level** defect, not a member fault.

**Consequence:** the Miidas row's **MISSED** verdict and §2's clause *"the daily self-audit
missed its 09-19 slot"* are **wrong on the cause**, corrected to: the job is armed, its slot
had not yet arrived, and the artifact absence traces to the declared writer's `--stamp`
invocation. **The `A2 = 2` reading and the Optimizing → Scalable band move stand** — the
undisputed fact is that no Tier-1 artifact exists for the 09-18 slot — but **§8 advisory 🔴 2
is retracted**: *"restore to daily execution"* is not the fix, because the job never stopped.

**The timezone VALUE is deliberate — the PREDICATE is the defect.** Miidas's own law states
the choice: `/root/miidas/SKILL.md:279` reads
`| miidas-hq-daily-trigger | 0 9 * * * UTC (= 12:00 MSK) |`. The fleet's stated client
expectation is this procedure's own §2 — `docs/measurement-procedure.md:31`, *"Sets
expectations for timing (daily at 09:00 MSK)"*. So 09:00 MSK is the reference and Miidas sits
three hours later **by its own written declaration**. A member's declared cadence is that
factory's call (§3 boundary), so whether that column moves to the reference is **not this
run's question and not a defect** — Miidas has put it to the owner itself. **Nothing in this
correction normalizes that column.**

What *is* this run's question is the **predicate**. `docs/measurement-procedure.md` §4 says
*"verify live execution against declared cadence"* and says nothing about per-row timezones.
The predicate applied from the next run is therefore: **freshness is judged against each job's
OWN last elapsed slot, read WITH its timezone** — not wall-clock "today" in UTC — and the
artifact-date proxy is **demoted to a secondary signal**, not the primary one. The structural
case is intrinsic to this run and independent of Miidas: `factory-measurement-daily` itself
fires `0 9 * * *` Europe/Moscow = **06:00Z** (live `last_run_at` 2026-09-19T06:01:32Z), exactly
at the stated expectation — so **any job whose slot falls after 06:00Z is measured before its
own slot arrives**, which is what happened to Miidas's 09:00Z slot.

*Authority, stated precisely:* procedure §2 names `Surveys` the **Process Owner**, accountable
for the audit specification — so this lane applies the corrected predicate to its own runs from
here. **Codifying the text is HQ's**, and is requested there: procedure §4 should carry the
timezone-aware own-slot comparison, so the rule stops depending on a lane remembering it.

*One observation, recorded and not scored* (their HQ's call, not this run's): Miidas's law row
declares `set_goal: true` for `miidas-hq-daily-trigger`, while the live cron row reads
`set_goal=0` — a law-vs-live drift on the thinness setting, independent of the timezone value.
