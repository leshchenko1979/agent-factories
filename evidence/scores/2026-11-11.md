## Method

Population: six factories declared in `registry/factories/*.json`. Criteria: the 19 cells of
`docs/quality-criteria.md`, scored 0–4. Fleet total is the sum of the six per-factory totals; each
factory's total is the sum of its own six family re-adds; each family re-add is the sum of its own
cells. Every one of the six tables above was checked to sum to its own printed re-add this round,
and the six totals were checked to sum to 350 (`71 + 38 + 79 + 64 + 57 + 41`).

Member state was read from each factory's **own** ledger at
`<repo>/evidence/ledger.jsonl`, parsed row-by-row for `event == "close"` and for rows inside the
seven-day window `2026-11-04T06:10Z … 2026-11-11T06:10Z`. Ledger paths read this round:
`/root/agent-factories` (meta-factory, 4 961 rows), `/root/inferhub-watch` (1 421),
`/root/vds-servers` (infra-factory, 1 980), `/root/miidas` (402), `/root/ai-antispam` (417).
`/root/opencrabs` and `/root/opencrabs-dev-factory` were both checked and neither carries an
`evidence/ledger.jsonl`.

**Every figure carries the instant it was read**, and every arithmetic result in this artifact was
computed through `python3` in the round that prints it — no figure is carried from a prior
artifact, and the two carried comparisons that appear above (`38 → 32` closes, `30.28 → 13.60`
