## Consulting Advisories (prioritised)

### 🔴 1. Meta-factory — the dispatch leg reports success without reading its own send

- **Observed symptom:** the daily measurement duty produced no artifact on 09-20 or 09-21 while
  `cron_job_runs` recorded `success` on both days. Two days of the factory's own measurement
  record are missing (§1).
- **Underlying mechanism:** the trigger row is a **thin trigger** — by design, and correctly so
  under P7. It backgrounds `opencrabs … session notify` with `nohup … &` and prints
  `Dispatched.` **without reading what the CLI wrote**. The CLI's failure went to a `/tmp` log.
  A thin row that hands off via a shell command has no channel back from the handoff to the
  scheduler.
- **Recommended process fix:** the notify-receipt leg landed under `#122` now patrols exactly
  this class, and `#139` carries its first live output. The residual gap is on the **trigger**
  side: the prompt should read the log file it just wrote and surface the CLI's own verdict in
  the run row. Until it does, the receipt lives in a patrol rather than in the scheduler.
- **Expected impact:** a failed dispatch becomes visible in `cron_job_runs` on the day it
  happens, instead of never.

### 🔴 2. Fleet — subject matter specification (D1) clears the consulting gate at the floor, four times

- **Observed symptom:** `D1 = 2.33` is the lowest criterion in the rubric and has not moved in
  four runs. Four of six factories score 2/4 on a criterion whose own anchor, `docs/subject/`, is
  **absent from every one of them** (census in §Subject Matter Consulting Gate Status).
- **Underlying mechanism:** the gate is satisfied on *substance* (domain grounding does exist —
  in `ONTOLOGY.md`, `skills/`, `probe/`, flat `docs/`) while the criterion's **named artefact** is
  missing. A criterion graded on substance while anchored on a path will keep reading 2 forever.
- **Recommended process fix:** a ruling on which it is — either the four factories create
  `docs/subject/`, or the rubric's anchor is widened to admit the artefact each actually keeps.
  Doing neither leaves every factory one regression from being barred from consulting.
- **Expected impact:** margin above the floor, and a criterion that moves when work is done.

### 🔴 3. OpenCrabs dev — the state repo accumulates uncommitted state (Recoverability 4 → 3)

- **Observed symptom:** at the read, `opencrabs-dev` carries **345 untracked entries** and
  **4 unpushed commits** (HEAD `eca2694`, `origin/main` `3ed0b72`), 13 modified tracked files.
- **Underlying mechanism:** the harvest machinery writes dated `.bak` sidecars
  (`*-harvest-state.md.2026-09-2xT*.bak`) and cursors (`.oc-harvest-dispatch-lane-cursor`) into
  the state repo, and nothing reaps them. Unlike the member repos, this surface has no hygiene
  reaper in its cycle.
- **Recommended process fix:** declare the generated patterns as a namespace and reap them on the
  existing hygiene cadence, as the member factories' `tools/hygiene.py` does for scratch.
