## Method

### Instrument

`python3 tools/audit.py --json` run detached in its own scope
(`audit-tree-1935450-1794549679.scope`, ceiling `2560M`, `MemorySwapMax=0`), launched
`06:01:19Z`, payload `/tmp/audit-2026-11-13.json`. **A peer audit was running concurrently**
(`/root/ai-antispam`, `pid 1921022`, `audit.py --report`, started ~`05:51Z`) on the same four
cores; load average read `2.96` at launch and `5.64` at `06:06:56Z`. **Every duration in the
self-audit verdict below is therefore taken under contention and is an upper bound**, not a
comparable measurement against an uncontended round.

All other figures come from the surface that owns them: systemd for units, the filesystem for
sizes, `tools/ledger.py` for ledger state, `tools/subject_anchor.py` for the documentation gate,
`tools/brain_metrics.py` for the context budget, and each factory's own `evidence/ledger.jsonl`
for per-day counts.

### Exclusivity

One writer per surface, per the state table in the factory's law. This artifact is the only
writer of `evidence/scores/2026-11-13.md`. Nothing here edits a member factory's state, and no
figure here is a copy of a member's own published number — where a member publishes its own
score, that number is theirs and this artifact's is this round's reading.

### What was re-read rather than carried

- **Every 7-day close count** was re-read from each factory's own ledger, and the per-day
  distribution was read as well — which is what turned five apparent regressions into one
  boundary effect. A total alone could not have done that.
- **`mac-cdp-tunnel`'s unit state** was re-read as a **system** unit after the `--user` read
  returned a plausible-looking zero.
- **The `opencrabs.service` cgroup** was read after the restart as well as before it, which is
  why the `memory.events` reset is recorded as a reset and not as a clean path.

### Four errors of this round, recorded

1. `cron_jobs` has no `schedule` column. It is `cron_expr` plus `timezone`. The first read of the
   pacemaker table failed on the missing column.
2. **A `--user` read of `mac-cdp-tunnel` returned `NRestarts=0 inactive`.** It is a system unit.
   The false negative is recorded as advisory 3's trap because it reads exactly like a fix.
3. **Three populations answered "how many skill bodies are over 500 lines"**: `wc -l
   skills/*/SKILL.md` (33 bodies), `find -maxdepth 3` (28), and `brain_metrics.py` leg B (1). The
   procedure's population is the profile's depth-1 set, 33. The other two are recorded in
   advisory 9 rather than silently dropped, because a count without its population is the defect.
4. `insights.py status` needs `--file`; the bare verb reports a usage error rather than a status.
   And this artifact briefly carried a hand-assembled repository handle (`leshchenko7497`) in
   advisory 5 — corrected to `leshchenko1979` before the artifact was committed. An identifier
   typed rather than copied is exactly what the standing rule forbids, and it is recorded here
   rather than quietly fixed.

### Two traps avoided

- **Reading the tunnel's restart count with `--user`** would have reported a 4 023-restart unit
  as fixed and idle. It was caught only because the restart count fell to zero, which is the
  shape of a fix — and the shape of a fix is not a fix.
- **Reading `6 → 4` closes as a throughput collapse.** It is a boundary effect, and the
  difference is 13 closes on one aged-out day.
