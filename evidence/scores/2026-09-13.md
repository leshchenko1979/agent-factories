## Implied Template Law & Cross-Factory Learnings

1. **A score movement requires a gate, not prose.** Meta-factory +11 and Miidas
   +2 both came from mechanical surfaces (test gates, CI-passed suites); AI
   AntiSpam's comprehensive codification moved 0 because nothing it added
   produces a number. Template consequence: the Day-1 Mandatory tier of
   `quality-criteria.md` is really a *gate-building* tier — bootstrap should
   name the first gate per mandated criterion (ontology test, single-writer
   verify, rework/defect log), not the document that describes the law.
   Reaffirms the 09-12 finding that Day-1 Mandatory criteria carry the highest
   leverage.

2. **A topic-bound lane is a durable asset; reuse beats respawn.** Miidas topic
   56 carried #25 → #26 on one session and is held idle for the next work unit;
   the meta-factory's delegate was lost once as a one-shot subagent. The template
   already codifies lane mechanics (`ad03ae2`); the newer, cheaper half — hold a
   closed lane for reuse instead of draining it — is worth stating explicitly in
   `TEMPLATE/roles/worker.md` or the delegation section.

3. **Every surveyed factory now has a clock.** Miidas (#22 thin-trigger daily),
   AI AntiSpam (triage-sweep hourly + digest daily), InferHub (hourly + daily
   report) and OpenCrabs (waiter sweep) all run a scheduled cycle that wakes
   their HQ/Triage without an owner tap. The template's minimal-factory list
   should include "a scheduled cycle that wakes the HQ lane" as a day-one item —
   the machines that advanced this run were the ones whose law was attached to a
   clock or a gate.
